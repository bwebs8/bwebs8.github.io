Who We Are

A Web Development Company Built Around What You Actually Need

B-Webs is a web development company in India that helps businesses, schools, communities and individuals build a professional presence online. In simple terms — you have an idea, and B-Webs turns it into a website.

Rather than forcing every client into the same template, we build around what each project actually needs — whether that's a simple business website, a portfolio, a full online store, or something more custom like a school site or a community platform with its own admin dashboard.

What We Focus On

  • Modern, current design — not something that looks outdated on day one
  • Fast-loading, lightweight websites
  • Mobile-friendly layouts that work properly on any screen
  • Professional, easy-to-use structure for visitors
  • Sensible pricing for small and growing businesses
  • Customized to each client rather than one fixed template

Who We Work With

Built for a Wide Range of Clients

We work with small businesses like shops, restaurants, and service providers; schools that need admissions and gallery pages; gaming communities that need rankings and team pages; developers, designers, and photographers who need a portfolio; and individuals who just want a professional presence online.
