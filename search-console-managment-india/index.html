What's Included

A Complete Google Search Console Management Service

Search Console is where Google tells you what's actually happening with your site — what's indexed, what's broken, and what people search to find you. Most businesses either never set it up, or set it up once and never check it again. We handle both.

Account Setup & Verification

We set up Google Search Console for your domain and complete ownership verification.

Sitemap Submission

Your sitemap gets submitted so Google has a clear map of every page on your site.

Indexing Requests

New or updated pages get submitted for indexing instead of waiting for Google to find them on its own.

Coverage & Error Monitoring

We keep an eye on crawl errors, mobile usability issues, and pages that fail to get indexed.

Performance Tracking

Monitoring which search queries actually bring people to your site, and how that changes over time.

Ongoing Fixes

When Search Console flags a problem, we address it instead of letting it sit.
